                                                                                             4.1. Positional encoders

                                                                                                The position and the order of segments are of great importance since
                                                                                             they define the temporal coherence of the signal. RNNs are regularly
                                                                                             used in NLP and they work by parsing a sentence word by word in a
                                                                                             sequential manner, which takes the order of the words into account. In
                                                                                             the case of TNNs, however, all the words of a sentence are processed
                                                                                             simultaneously. It is therefore important to incorporate some sense of
                                                                                             order into the TNN model. The process of positional encoding consists
                                                                                             of adding a piece of information about the word’s position to each
                                                                                             word in the sentence. To continue the analogy that we have already
                                                                                             applied to our work, the current-signal window behaves like a sentence
                                                                                             and each segment of this window can be compared to a word in that
                                                                                             sentence. In this paper, we have used, as a positional encoder, a layer
                                                                                             that learns the positional vectors of each signal segment. We call this
                                                                                             layer the ‘embedding layer’.
